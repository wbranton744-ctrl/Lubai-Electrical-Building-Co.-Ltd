About Lubai Ltd

Engineered for absolute reliability, safety, and modern infrastructure excellence across commercial, industrial, and residential sectors in Kenya.

Who We Are

Powering Kenya’s Growth with Precision Engineering

Lubai Ltd is a premier electrical engineering and building infrastructure contractor based in Nairobi. We specialize in end-to-end electrical distribution, structured installations, renewable solar energy integration, and integrated facility security systems.

Driven by a commitment to technical excellence and strict regulatory compliance, our certified engineers handle projects of all scales—from complex industrial power sub-stations to high-end commercial tower grids and perimeter security deployments.
